M.R AI 0.10.0 — Internal QA

Added optional browser notifications for task reminders. Reminders are checked on page load and every 30 seconds while the page is active. Browser notification permission is requested only after a user click. A reminder is marked as notified after successful Notification construction to avoid repeated alerts.

Limitations: no guaranteed background delivery; no OS-level scheduling; no push server; no Android device test; browser support and permission vary.
