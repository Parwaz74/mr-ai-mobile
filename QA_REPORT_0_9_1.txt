M.R AI 0.9.1 — internal review notes

Changes in this increment:
- Added a visible, accessible notice when a selected language has no complete UI translation.
- Kept the 39-language selector and the English fallback behavior. Full translations remain available only for English, Persian and Arabic.
- Improved voice recognition status messages for empty recognition and recognition completion.
- Voice recognition remains manual: recognized text is placed in the composer and is never submitted automatically.
- Updated displayed build version and backup filename.

Validation to run:
  node --check app.js
  node --check server.mjs
  node --test tests/*.test.cjs

Limitations:
- No Android device/browser microphone test was performed in this environment.
- Browser speech recognition and speech synthesis availability varies by browser, OS and locale.
- No live model provider is configured; config.js keeps apiEnabled false.
- Full translation of all 39 languages is not complete.
- No real image, video or music generation engine is connected.
